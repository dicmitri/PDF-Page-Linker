PDF Page and Passage Links: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026 (version 2.0)

PDF Page and Passage Links copies links to a page or passage of a PDF you are reading. Everything it does happens on your device. It has no servers, no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it never sends anything you read or copy to its developer or to anyone else.

What the extension reads

Only when you choose one of its items from the right-click menu, or press its keyboard shortcut, the extension receives the text you selected and the address of the PDF. To find which page that text is on, it downloads that same PDF from the website you are viewing, just as your browser did to display it, and extracts the PDF's text on your device. The download uses your browser's existing cookies for that website, so PDFs you can already see keep working.

The PDF's text is kept in memory for up to three recently used PDFs, so further links are instant. It is never written to disk or sent anywhere, and it is discarded when the browser closes.

The extension does not read pages you haven't asked it to, and it has no permanent access to any website.

What is stored on your device

Each link you copy is saved in the extension's local storage (chrome.storage.local) with:

  • the PDF's address and file name;
  • the page number;
  • the copied link;
  • the date and time you copied it;
  • for passage and quote links, the selected text (up to 240 characters).

The result of the most recent right-click, including the selected text, is also kept in session storage (chrome.storage.session) so the popup can show it. Chrome clears session storage when the browser closes.

Network access

The only network request the extension makes is downloading the PDF you right-clicked in, from the website it came from. It contacts no other server.

Deleting your data

Use Delete next to a link in the extension's popup to remove it from the history. Removing the extension deletes all of its stored data.

Permissions

  • contextMenus: show the right-click items.
  • activeTab: temporary access to the current tab's website, granted only when you click one of the extension's items or press its shortcut, used to download the PDF.
  • offscreen: run the PDF text extraction in a hidden extension page.
  • scripting: copy the link to your clipboard from the current tab, and read the selected text when you press the keyboard shortcut.
  • storage: keep your link history on your device.

Third parties

The extension bundles the open-source PDF.js library by Mozilla to read PDF text locally. PDF.js runs inside the extension and does not contact any server.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here with a new date. Version 1 of the extension stored the same history fields, except the selected text.
